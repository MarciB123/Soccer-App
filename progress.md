## PROJECT CONTEXT

### What It Is
An AI-powered soccer training app for iOS. Users go through an onboarding questionnaire, get a personalized training plan generated by Coach Alex (Claude AI), and can chat with their AI coach. Coach Alex assigns specific tasks, the player completes them and reports back. The plan adapts based on completion history.

### Who It's For
- Primary: Youth players aged 13–17 in the USA
- Secondary: Young adults 17–25
- All competition levels from recreational to pro
- COPPA flow handles under-13 users (parental consent required)

### Solo Founder
One developer building and launching this alone. App is pre-launch, in beta.

### Monetization
- **$25.99/month** (set in App Store Connect only — no code change needed)
- 7-day free trial
- No paywall during initial beta testing
- RevenueCat handles all billing

## PRODUCT VISION — ALIGNED MARCH 10, 2026

### How Training Works
- Coach Alex assigns specific tasks/drills each week
- Player completes them and reports back (marks done, rates difficulty)
- **Required drills: 3–4 soccer-specific drills per week (must complete these first)**
- **Optional drills unlock AFTER required drills are done:** cardio, abs, juggling, etc.
- Plan adapts based on last 4 weeks of completion history (rolling adaptive memory)
- Training days per week is set by user but no specific days locked in
- Auto-reminder: "You've trained X times this week, you have X left — train today to hit your goal"

### Per-Drill Feedback (To Build)
- Easy / Medium / Hard rating per drill
- Optional comment per drill
- Stored in `drill_completions` table (Option B — chosen)
- Session-level star rating (1–5) stays as-is after "Finish Session"

### Competition Levels (To Replace Skill Level)
Replacing current skill_level field with competition_level:
- Recreational
- High School
- Club / Academy
- College
- Semi-Pro
- Pro

### Coach Alex Tour (To Build)
- Runs ONCE on first login after signup — controlled by `has_seen_tour` boolean in profiles table
- Automated scripted messages (NOT live AI) — pulls user's name from profile
- ~5 messages covering: what app does, how training works, how streak works, how to talk to Coach Alex
- Script to be written together before building
- Fixed opening line direction: "Hey [Name], my goal is to get you fully skilled and developed..."
- After tour: CTA goes to home screen with training button highlighted

### Help Button (To Build)
- Lives in profile tab
- Opens static screen with 5 most common questions answered
- Has "Message Coach Alex" shortcut at the bottom
- No AI — just static text, fast and reliable

## PRE-PAYWALL "PLAN READY" SCREEN — DESIGN LOCKED IN

Based on research from Reddit, RevenueCat, Adapty, Superwall, and successful fitness apps.

**Structure:**
1. Loading animation (2–3 sec): "Analyzing your profile and building your plan with Coach Alex..."
2. Headline: "[Name], your personalized training plan is ready"
3. Summary card showing:
   - Their goal (from onboarding)
   - Sessions per week (from their selection)
   - Week 1 focus (e.g. "Ball control + weak foot finishing")
   - Projected milestone: "Players at your level training Xx/week typically see measurable improvement in 30 days"
4. Week 1 locked preview: Session 1 visible, Sessions 2–4 locked with lock icon
5. Social proof: "Join 12,000+ youth players training with Coach Alex"
6. CTA: "Unlock My Plan" or "Start My Free Trial" — NOT "Subscribe"

**Rules (DO NOT violate):**
- Never show a generic feature list ("Unlimited workouts, AI chat...")
- Never paywall before user has felt any value
- Never use aggressive pop-ups mid-experience
- Never hide the user's own data behind the paywall
- Always use the user's first name on this screen (+17% conversion)

**Key data from research:**
- Personalized plan preview: up to +65% conversion lift
- Adding user's name: +17%
- 7-day trial converts at 45.7% trial-to-paid
- 80% of trial starts happen Day 0 (same session as install)
